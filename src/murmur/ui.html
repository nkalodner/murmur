Hotkeys

Hold to dictate; a quick tap locks hands-free; Esc cancels. Two independent keys, and either can be a combination held together.

First hotkey
ctrl_r
Second hotkey
off

Bare modifiers like right Ctrl work best; a letter key would still type. The two keys cannot contain each other.
